✦ Core Features

Everything a Student Needs

Powerful tools to keep your attendance on track — from smart dashboards to exam day marking.

📊

Smart Dashboard

See today's full class schedule at a glance. Mark attendance with one tap. Dashboard auto-splits pending and marked lectures with live attendance percentage.

🧠

Prediction Engine

Know exactly how many classes you can safely skip. Get real-time predictions on safe bunks, classes needed, and end-of-semester projected attendance.

⚡

100% Offline First

Built on Isar NoSQL — the fastest Flutter database. All data lives on your phone. Zero internet required. Sub-100ms response times guaranteed.

📅

Calendar & Timetable

Set up your weekly timetable once and forget. The app auto-generates today's classes every day. View full attendance history on a color-coded calendar.

🎓

Exam Day Marking

Mark Mid Sem 1, Mid Sem 2, End Sem, and Practical Exam days. Exam days are intelligently excluded from regular attendance calculations automatically.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Timely reminders before each class. Attendance shortage alerts. Goal achievement celebrations. Auto daily backups keep your data safe.
