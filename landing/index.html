Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Attendify, attendance tracking, and bunk calculation.

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes Held) × 100. If you attended 42 out of 50 classes, your attendance is 84%. Attendify calculates this automatically per subject in real-time — no math needed.

To stay above 75% attendance: Safe Bunks = (Total Classes × 0.25) − Classes Already Missed. Attendify's Prediction Engine shows this number live for every subject, so you always know exactly how many classes you can skip without risking attendance shortage.

You need to attend at least 3 out of every 4 classes to maintain 75%. Attendify shows a real-time alert when you're approaching the danger zone and tells you exactly how many consecutive classes you must attend to recover your percentage.

100% free, forever. No account, no login, no subscription. Download the APK directly — no Play Store needed. All data stays on your phone. No data is ever sent to any server.

Yes — Attendify is 100% offline. Built on Isar NoSQL (the fastest Flutter database), all data lives on your phone. You can track attendance, view analytics, and use every feature with zero internet connection, even in your college building with no signal.

Attendify works on Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) and above. It's built with Flutter so it runs smoothly on both old and new Android devices. An iOS version is not available yet.

1. Click Download APK on this page. 2. Open your Android Settings → Security → Enable "Install from unknown sources". 3. Open the downloaded APK file and tap Install. Done — Attendify is ready to use in under a minute.

A bunk calculator tells you how many classes you can skip while keeping your attendance above a minimum threshold (usually 75%). Attendify's built-in bunk predictor does this per subject, in real-time, so you never accidentally cross the line.
